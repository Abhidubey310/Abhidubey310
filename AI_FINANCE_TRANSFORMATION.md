# How I Use AI in Finance Transformation

AI is most useful to me when it helps turn a finance problem into a working, controlled solution faster.

In my current finance transformation work, I use tools including **Claude and Claude Code** to support solution design, rapid prototyping, rule development, testing and documentation. I do not treat AI as a replacement for finance judgement or controls.

## Where I use it

Typical use cases include:

- month-end and reconciliation automation;
- bank reconciliation and transaction matching;
- GL and journal workflow automation;
- billing and revenue-process automation;
- cash and treasury workflows;
- Finance / FinOps reporting;
- exception investigation and root-cause analysis; and
- recurring operational tasks where rules, controls and evidence can be made more consistent.

## My delivery approach

**Finance problem → current process → control requirements → AI-assisted design/build → validation → UAT → productionisation → measurable outcome**

### 1. Start with the finance problem

Before building anything, I define the business issue, process owner, expected outcome, control requirements and where human judgement is still required.

### 2. Translate requirements into structured logic

I break the process into:

- source data and required fields;
- business rules;
- exception scenarios;
- ownership and escalation;
- expected outputs;
- approval points; and
- reconciliation/control checks.

### 3. Use Claude / Claude Code as a build accelerator

I use AI to help convert those requirements into working prototypes, scripts, validation logic, test scenarios, workflow ideas and documentation.

The important point is that the finance logic comes first. AI accelerates the build; it does not define the accounting policy or make an uncontrolled financial decision.

### 4. Validate against the business rules

Outputs are reconciled back to source data and tested against expected scenarios. Exceptions are reviewed explicitly rather than hidden by automation.

### 5. Run UAT with Finance users

The solution is tested with the people who understand the process, including edge cases, controls, ownership and operational usability.

### 6. Productionise with appropriate technology controls

A production implementation may use ERP, CRM, workflow, cloud, reporting and approved enterprise-AI services. Access, logging, approvals and segregation of duties remain part of the design.

### 7. Measure the outcome

The final question is not whether AI was used. It is whether the change reduced manual effort, improved control, accelerated cycle time, improved data quality or gave Finance better visibility.

## What I keep human-controlled

I would not allow an AI assistant to independently:

- post or approve material journals;
- release payments;
- recognise revenue;
- change contractual terms;
- change critical master data;
- override approval limits; or
- close material finance exceptions without appropriate review.

AI can prepare, summarise, classify, suggest and accelerate. The controlled finance decision remains with the authorised person.
